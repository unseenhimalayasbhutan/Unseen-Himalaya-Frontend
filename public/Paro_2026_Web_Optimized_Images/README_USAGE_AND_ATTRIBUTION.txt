PARO 2026 — REAL PHOTO WEBSITE PACK
====================================

WHAT IS INSIDE
• originals/      Original high-resolution source files downloaded from verified reusable sources.
• web_optimized/  Website-ready JPEG copies, max 2400 px on the long edge, quality 88, no cropping or AI processing.
• PHOTO_CREDITS_AND_LICENSES.csv  Photographer, source page, license and exact attribution text for every included image.
• WEBSITE_IMAGE_MAP_ALL_PARO_ATTRACTIONS.csv  Maps the full Paro attraction list to included assets and flags gaps.
• NOT_INCLUDED_RIGHTS_REVIEW.csv  Attractions deliberately excluded because commercial reuse rights were not clear enough.
• Paro_Photo_Contact_Sheet.jpg  Visual index of included images.

IMPORTANT RIGHTS NOTES
1. All bundled photographs are real photographs. No generative-AI image has been used.
2. This pack intentionally excludes Getty/iStock/Alamy-type rights-managed photos, unclear-copyright images, and Creative Commons “NonCommercial” (BY-NC) images.
3. CC BY and CC BY-SA images may be used commercially when you comply with the license conditions. Attribution is required.
4. For CC BY-SA images, adaptations must be shared under the same or a compatible ShareAlike license. Simple website resizing/compression may count as a technical modification; keep the credit/license information with your project.
5. Best practice: put the credit directly below the image where practical, or create a clearly accessible “Photo Credits” page on your website. Use the exact credit text in PHOTO_CREDITS_AND_LICENSES.csv.
6. The farmhouse image is a representative Bhutanese farmhouse; do not present it as a named Paro property unless you independently verify the exact location.
7. Haa Valley is not in Paro. Use the Haa image only under “Day Trips / Excursions from Paro.”
8. If your website remains online for years, re-open the source page before major redesign/republication to preserve an audit trail of the license.

EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION
Use the 'web_optimized' files on the website for faster loading. Keep 'originals' as your master archive. Do not unnecessarily upload the 20–30 MB originals to production.
